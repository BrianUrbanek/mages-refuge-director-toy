V16 -- last-charge strategies, support screens and scripted floor-boss stats

Shared throughput rules remain: spend with 2+ charges, and spend a lone charge
when another is within two seconds of recharge. The additional last-charge
condition is selected independently of progression: Relieve pressure (<=50% HP,
default), Prevent overwhelm (>=20 living fuel-equivalent), Priority targets
(support or glass-cannon tag present). Priority changes timing, not targeting.
Glass-cannon recognition is available; current content has supports and does not
yet author a separate glass-cannon HP/DPS profile.

Supports absorb fire before all other units, for baseline/charged pulses and
spill. Manual selections and nearest/elite policies operate within that screen.
Spill damage remains conserved. The redundant Support-first choice is removed
from settings; legacy API value remains accepted. No support aura mechanics.

Floor lieutenants and final/Sanctum bosses use 35 fuel-equivalent: 770 HP and
8.4 incoming DPS at current coefficients. They introduce/spend zero Director
fuel. Linked guardians retain 4 equivalent. Living-pressure triggers include
scripted equivalent; ledger accounting and defeat-fuel potion refill do not.

Strategy changes apply live, clear prior emergency intent, persist on reset,
and appear in results, full trace and copied Journal. Default player unchanged.
The stronger floor bosses change balance: default Steady now fails the baseline
required route. Prior default-win assertion was replaced with honest outcome and
conservation checks. All-profile completion fixture now explicitly uses 72 DPS,
200 HP and 3 potions; this does not retune the default player.

221 checks plus module syntax pass, including nine new focused checks.
Director core remains unchanged; production/C# integration remains untested.
