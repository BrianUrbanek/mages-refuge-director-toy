eligible composed option. Preserve the selected core; enforce hard pack limits.

compose an affordable terminal pack; unspendable residue remains recorded.
budgetComplete means no next fundable group, not no living opposition. New fuel

zone. Boss arrival preserves ledger and N+1. Entry zone has no scripted leader.
