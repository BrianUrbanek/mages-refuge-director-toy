V14 -- forecast-aware player approaches and concise combat Journal

Demo passive-funding warning lead is six seconds, injected by the game host.
The extractable Director core default remains configurable and otherwise unchanged.
Funding/readiness still dispatch immediately; warning does not add a spawn delay.
Sealed standalone breaches remain unannounced. Normal use after opening warns.

Steady and Press do not auto-advance while an outstanding forecast exists.
Their existing clear/HP/charge requirements still apply. Cancellation unblocks
movement; Charge and deliberate manual movement can still invite overlap.

Telemetry has a separate CombatSummary aggregator. It starts when opposition
arrives, merges overlapping groups, and emits exactly once at the first empty
instant before any leader callback can move/spawn. An interrupted fight is
labeled overwhelmed/returned, never invented as a clear. Details include time,
health lost after passive regen, start/end HP, kills, groups/fuel, scripted leaders,
peak pressure, bursts and potions. Potion healing cannot conceal health loss.

The visible Journal hides per-sample HP, pulse/charge and passive-tick entries.
Those raw entries remain in the full JSON export and timeline. Arrival, room,
funding event, potion, Director decision and result entries remain visible.
Copy log copies the full concise log as readable timestamped text, regardless
of the active filter/window; failed clipboard access reports use Export instead.

200 current checks pass, including 12 new V14 regressions. Core tests remain
unchanged. V10's no-forecast-veto assertion was updated to the new user rule.
No C#/Last Epoch integration or production asset-load performance validation.
