MAGE'S REFUGE — ENCOUNTER DIRECTOR V5

Extractable core: dist/core/encounter-manager.mjs and its TypeScript declaration.
No DOM, rendering, combat, room or zone model in the core. Host supplies time,
resolved content, budget/release messages, portal descriptors and spawn outcomes.

BOUNDARY
Zone activation: applyOnce(uniqueEventId, {add: zoneBudget}).
First room entry: host tracks entry once, calls transfer(amount, zoneSource),
then requestSpawn(). This releases existing fuel and adds no room budget.
Zone profile: updateConfig(config), including its passive flow rate.
Portal list: updatePortals([{id, tags, eligible}]). No positions or geometry.
Director preferences are ordered tags. corePortalPreferences can override them
for a particular authored group core. Prepared N+1 retains its resolved portal
preferences across menu changes. Portal selection occurs at dispatch, not prep.
Eligible portals passing host canEnter are ranked lexicographically by ordered
tag preference, with stable ID tie-breaks. Unmatched tags permit fallback.
Sealed-side is a descriptive tag; eligible determines usability. Zone may stage
a door-opening arrival. The demo unlocks that capability in the third room.
Host receives commitSpawn(group, selectedPortalId); it cannot substitute another
portal. Atomic success returns one distinct actor ID per member. Failure cleans
up hidden partial actors and spends nothing. Warning cancellation retains N+1
and the request; new portal list can allow a different selection.
Normal profile updates preserve N+1. Explicit invalidation is separate.
Fuel commits once only after successful arrival. Host reports defeats by ID.

DEMO LAYER
dist/zone-host.mjs owns zone budgets, room entry state and portal descriptors.
dist/simulation.mjs owns abstract combat, physical spawn execution, progression.
dist/visualization.mjs receives detached snapshots and owns visual positions.
dist/app.mjs owns UI and browser-local reward storage.
Each zone has four rooms and 100 points at depth 1. Budget/prices scale by depth.
Room entry releases 25, or enough for an already committed larger group when
remaining zone Potential allows. Every entry is one-time. Passive flow may
consume the entire activated zone before later rooms are entered. Later rooms
then release zero; no hidden replacement budget or forced wave is invented.
Normal targets 15–40 points; balanced stage targets 18/20/22/25. Different profiles
choose forward, behind, open-side or sealed-side; Champion cores prefer sealed.
Boss/end exception releases all remaining Potential, cancels old commitment and
buys affordable packs/ad hoc units, leaving sub-cheapest residue unused.
Room Finished is host-owned historical quiet observation, not an ED lifecycle
and not a progression permission. Enemies and fuel remain globally shared.

SIMULATION PASS V5
The encounter core is unchanged. Authored flow is 0.5 points/sec; autonomous
timing is now governed by funding; see the V8 correction below.
Combat is host-only: fixed 36 damage/sec, pulse every 0.5s. Enemy HP = cost*22,
including Champions (no extra Champion health multiplier). Scripted lieutenant,
final boss, Sanctum boss and Guardian costs are 12, 18, 24 and 4 respectively.
Incoming player damage = total living cost*0.24 HP/sec, including supports.
Player regenerates 1 HP/sec, capped at 100. No healing button or manual burst.
Two starting charges; one regenerated every 10s while below cap; no extra reserve.
Spend one per pulse when incoming damage minus regen >= 2*regen. The entire
pulse is 4 times passive damage, not an independently tuned attack or AoE nova.
Excess damage spills to nearest logical position, with stable actor-ID ties.
Simulation owns logical actor positions; renderer only displays detached copies
and visual separation. Every hit carries beam endpoints, including lethal hits.
Simulation advances internally in 0.05s steps regardless of browser frame delta.
Steady: quiet/unaffordable current arrival, at least 98 HP, both charges.
Press: <=4 living points, at least 82 HP and one charge, no pending warning.
Charge: no reserve checks; 2s room dwell. Default balanced charging dies from
overlap, while steady and press finish required and full routes after resting.
The 20-point controlled example clears in 9.5s with two bursts vs 12.5s without.
Baseline power UI is a readout; power/tankiness sliders and UI trays are deferred.
History/export include HP, living cost, charges, burst count and rest time.

V17 -- REMAINING BUDGET AND BOSS GO-TIME (supersedes boss flush / 35-point rules)
The demo opts into Director runtime budgetAwareComposition:true. Core default
false retains legacy consumers/golden vectors; an extracting host should enable
this option for the current design. Composition is capped by Available+Potential.
Only newly composed groups are resized; frozen N+1 remains frozen. Expandable
groups absorb a projected remaining tail smaller than the cheapest currently
eligible composed option. Preserve the selected core; enforce hard pack limits.
Transfer half the actual added composition cost immediately, capped by Potential.
Standalone breaches never inflate. If no listed recipe fits remaining balance,
compose an affordable terminal pack; unspendable residue remains recorded.
budgetComplete means no next fundable group, not no living opposition. New fuel
resumes preparation. Missing portal/content opportunities are errors, not clears.
beginFinalSpend remains a legacy explicit API; bosses no longer call it.

Boss timing belongs to ZoneHost/BossClock, outside the Director. Demo defaults:
long 10s after last-room entry with living opposition; short 6s after BOTH no
living actors and no scheduled/fundable groups, in ANY room. A lull is insufficient.
Completing during a long timer subtracts long-short (4s) from its old deadline;
it never starts a fresh six seconds and can become immediately due. One boss per
zone. Boss arrival preserves ledger and N+1. Entry zone has no scripted leader.
Boss stats: 20 equivalent, 550 HP, damageMultiplier .8 -> baseline DPS 3.84;
zero Director fuel cost. Guardians remain 4 equivalent, 88 HP, .96 DPS.
Default targeting and spill: supports first, then lowest remaining HP, stable ID
ties. Explicit clicks/elite policy still choose the initial eligible target.
Scripted arrivals are stated in Journal. Display-only gold halo identifies bosses.
Production Last Epoch integration and JavaScript-to-C# translation remain untested.
